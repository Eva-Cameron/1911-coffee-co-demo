## Menu (as published by the café)

- **Printed brunch menu** (in their latte photo, 22 Oct 2026): American buttermilk pancakes, served until 11:00 —
  just syrup £7; + bacon £2, + toasted pecans £2, + blueberries £2. Add some extras: black pudding £1.50,
  hash brown £1.00. Bacon or sausage sandwich. "If you have a food allergy please make this known when ordering."
  Other lines are hidden under the cup.
- **Lunch post** (Facebook, 15 Apr 2026): cold sandwiches, paninis, warm baguettes, all served with a fresh side
  salad, homemade coleslaw and red cabbage. Soup of the day, always gluten free. A children's menu.
- **Customer favourites** (Restaurantji): The 1911 Breakfast, bacon or sausage sandwich, English breakfast,
  beans on toast, egg on toast, egg sandwich, baguette, scone. Reviews mention a coronation chicken warm
  baguette and homemade scones and cakes (not used as claims).
- **Photos** show: soup with roll and butter; toasted fruit bread with butter and jam; salad plates; cakes on the
  counter in front of the espresso machine; a bacon and hash brown bun.

## Colour

Three directions were tested against the photographs and for contrast:

1. **Postcard** (from the brief): cream ground; mint, blush, custard and sky fields; cherry red for actions; espresso ink.
2. **Shopfront**: the shop's own orange and pilaster grey with maroon ink.
3. **Harbour**: pale sky and navy with custard.

Chosen: **Postcard, with the shop's orange kept for the opening scene** — the orange is the real brand colour,
and it makes the first screen unmistakably theirs. Contrast: espresso ink is 5.5:1 or better on every field;
cherry is used as text only on cream and custard (5.8:1, 4.75:1), never on mint, blush or sky.
